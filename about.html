About MOMe

Empowering mothers through innovation and care

Our Story

MOMe was founded in 2023 with a simple but powerful vision: to transform the pregnancy experience by combining healthcare, financial planning, and community support into a single comprehensive platform.

After witnessing firsthand the challenges that expectant mothers face—from managing healthcare appointments to planning for the financial impact of a new family member—our founders set out to create a solution that addresses these pain points holistically.

Today, MOMe has grown into a trusted companion for thousands of mothers worldwide, guiding them through every step of their pregnancy journey with personalized support, expert resources, and innovative tools.

Our Mission

To simplify and enhance the pregnancy journey by providing integrated solutions that address healthcare, financial planning, and community support needs in one accessible platform.

Our Vision

A world where every expectant mother has the tools, resources, and support she needs to navigate pregnancy with confidence, joy, and peace of mind.

Our Core Values

Innovation

We continuously improve our platform with cutting-edge technology to better serve mothers' evolving needs.

Empowerment

We equip mothers with the knowledge and tools they need to make informed decisions about their health and future.

Inclusivity

We design our services to support mothers from all backgrounds, cultures, and circumstances.

Reliability

We provide trustworthy information and dependable services when mothers need them most.
